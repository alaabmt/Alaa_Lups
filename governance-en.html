TRANSPARENCY AND ACCOUNTABILITY

How is health education reviewed before publication?

NA7N is a patient education initiative operated by Tamayuz10x in Abu Dhabi. Lupus is our first pilot, with a long-term vision to support people living with other chronic diseases and their families. We do not diagnose, prescribe, or provide individual treatment decisions.

Implementation status: This page describes our governance framework and controls under development and verification. It does not signify government endorsement or that every published article has completed review. Only materials with documented approvals may be labelled medically reviewed.

Our educational content approval pathway

  1. Define patient and family needs

    Specify the topic, learning goal, intended audience, and clinical risk level.

  2. Collect and assess evidence

    Use relevant professional guidelines and research; document sources, dates, and the claims they support.

  3. Scientific editing and translation

    Prepare clear Arabic-first materials, verify medical terminology and fidelity to English sources, and check use rights.

  4. Safety and conflict-of-interest screening

    Check medication warnings, privacy, signed disclosures, and the professional licence of each reviewer.

  5. Two independent physician reviews of the same version

    Physician reviewer 1

    A physician with a current UAE professional licence assesses accuracy, evidence, and patient safety.

    Physician reviewer 2

    A different UAE-licensed physician independently evaluates the material. Appropriate medical review of the Arabic text is included.

    Each physician signs a role agreement and conflict-of-interest disclosure. A role agreement does not approve an article; each decision is recorded against the assigned version through the review system.

  6. Resolve comments and repeat review if changed

    Any material requiring changes or rejected by a reviewer is held. Material clinical edits trigger fresh review of the new version.

  7. Publication authorization and quality checks

    The authorized publisher checks the two distinct physician approvals, language, privacy, accessibility, and version matching before documenting publication.

  8. Ongoing monitoring

    Monitor new evidence, feedback and errors; correct or suspend materials where necessary while preserving an audit trail.

Our internal publication rule: a medically reviewed item requires two independent recorded approvals from different UAE-licensed physicians for the same version.

Protecting user information

UAE data-hosting roadmap

We plan to host patient databases, processing and backups in an appropriately controlled UAE environment after regulatory and technical assessment. Migration has not yet been confirmed complete.

Privacy and cybersecurity

We are evaluating data ownership, collection purposes, access permissions, consent, retention, deletion, account isolation, encryption, and incident response before handling real identifiable health information.

Relevant requirements

We will assess the applicability of UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2019, its implementing regulation, and DoH Abu Dhabi's ADHICS V2 standard to the services we actually provide.

Editorial independence

No sponsor or commercial partner may override clinical judgments. Patient stories, research participation and account access require distinct governance and permissions.

What can patients check?

We aim to show each item's sources, update date and verified review status, and reviewer names and credentials where the reviewers have explicitly consented. Version-specific approvals are retained in internal records.

Regulatory reference points

UAE Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 and DoH Abu Dhabi AAMEN programme. Referring to these does not mean NA7N has received accreditation or certification.
