Living with lupus

Life between appointments matters.

Living well with lupus includes managing fatigue, sleep, movement, sun exposure, nutrition, emotional wellbeing, work and study while keeping medical follow-up in perspective.

Important

This information is educational. It does not diagnose your condition or replace advice from your healthcare team.

Key information

Fatigue

Look for patterns rather than blaming yourself. Record sleep, activity, symptoms and other changes that may help your healthcare team consider possible causes.

Patient guide

Sleep

Regular sleep habits can support wellbeing, but persistent sleep problems deserve discussion because pain, mood, medicines and other conditions may contribute.

Patient guide

Movement

Appropriate physical activity can support strength, mobility and general health. The right level depends on your condition and should be adjusted when symptoms or organ involvement require it.

Patient guide

Food

There is no single diet that cures lupus. Aim for a balanced pattern unless your healthcare team recommends specific changes for kidney disease, medicines or another condition.

Patient guide

Emotional wellbeing, work and study

Chronic illness can affect mood, relationships, work and study. Practical adjustments and professional support can be part of lupus care when needed.

What should I do now?

Write down new or changing symptoms.Keep your medicines and recent results organized.Prepare your most important questions before your visit.Seek medical assessment promptly for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms.
