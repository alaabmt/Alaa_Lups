Mycophenolate

A major option in many lupus nephritis plans, requiring careful monitoring and pregnancy counseling.

Why might it be prescribed?

May be used for lupus kidney disease and other selected organ manifestations.

What makes this medicine different?

Important in several kidney treatment plans. Different formulations are absorbed differently and must not be substituted independently.

Before adding any medicineAsk your pharmacist to check interactions, including OTC medicines and supplements. Do not change treatment based on this page.

How it works: analogy and explanation
Think of the same factory: mycophenolate reduces a supply essential for certain immune cells to multiply. This differs from azathioprine's pathway.

It inhibits an enzyme needed to make building blocks used by lymphocytes for division.

The analogy is a learning aid, not a literal description of biology.

How is it used and when may it help?

Available as tablets, capsules and different formulations; food and timing instructions depend on the product. Follow the specific label.

If you miss a dose, follow the instructions for your specific product or ask your pharmacist. Do not double up without explicit advice.

Side effects and monitoring

Diarrhea, nausea or stomach upset may occur, with low blood counts and infection risk.

Regular blood tests and disease monitoring are important. Formulations are not automatically interchangeable.

Which medicines may interact?

Antacids containing magnesium or aluminum can reduce absorption of certain products; ask about formulation-specific spacing.

These are important examples, not a complete interaction list. Some combinations are intentional with monitoring; others may need avoidance. Bring your complete medicine list to your appointments.

Pregnancy, breastfeeding and family planning

Can cause miscarriage and major birth defects. Arrange contraception and pregnancy planning before use; contact the care team immediately if pregnancy is suspected.

When to get prompt or urgent medical help

Seek assessment for major infection symptoms, unexplained bleeding, or suspected pregnancy.

How does it compare with other medicines?
It can be intentionally combined with hydroxychloroquine. Azathioprine has a different role and is not an automatic substitute.
