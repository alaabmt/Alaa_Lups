Skin & sun

Protect your skin and document changes clearly.

Lupus can affect the skin, and ultraviolet exposure can worsen some skin manifestations or trigger symptoms in some people.

Important

This information is educational. It does not diagnose your condition or replace advice from your healthcare team.

Key information

Sun and ultraviolet exposure

Discuss appropriate sun protection with your healthcare team. Practical protection may include shade, protective clothing and suitable sunscreen.

Patient guide

Document a rash

Photographs can help show how a rash changes. Note when it began, where it appeared, whether it itches or hurts, sun exposure and any new medicines or products.

Patient guide

Not every rash is lupus

Rashes have many causes. A new skin change should not automatically be labeled a lupus flare.

Patient guide

Hair changes

Hair loss can occur for different reasons, including lupus activity, scalp inflammation, medicines or unrelated conditions. The pattern and timing matter.

Patient guide

When to seek assessment

Rapidly worsening, painful, blistering, infected-looking or otherwise concerning skin changes deserve clinical assessment.

What should I do now?

Write down new or changing symptoms.Keep your medicines and recent results organized.Prepare your most important questions before your visit.Seek medical assessment promptly for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms.
