Quality of life

What matters to you in your life?

Your priority might be having more usable energy, sleeping better, staying at work or study, spending time with family, moving in a way that suits you, or preparing for a new stage of life. The goal is not to monitor lupus all day; it is to protect what matters in your life.

Energy & fatigueSleepMovementWork & studyEmotional wellbeingPregnancy planning

Choose one priority to start with, then turn it into a question or goal to discuss at your next visit.

Partnership in care

Make your healthcare visit more useful.

Before the visit, organize what changed and what matters. During it, ask questions and understand the options. Before you leave, make sure you know the next step.

Safety first

When should I seek medical help?

If you develop new symptoms or a clear change in your health, do not rely on this site to diagnose the problem or decide how urgent it is. Follow guidance from your healthcare team, contact them when needed, and seek urgent or emergency care when the situation requires it.

We are developing more detailed guidance for clinical review. We will not use a questionnaire or self-reported score to diagnose lupus activity.
