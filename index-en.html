Safety first

Are you experiencing new or worsening symptoms?

This website provides education and cannot diagnose symptoms or determine urgency. Contact your healthcare team about new or concerning changes, and seek urgent or emergency care for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms. Never wait for an online tool result before seeking help.

Supported self-management

From knowledge to a practical next step

Self-management does not mean managing healthcare alone. It means understanding what you notice, what you can organize, and what needs discussion with your healthcare team.

Partnership in care

Make your healthcare visit more useful.

Before the visit, organize what changed and what matters. During it, ask questions and understand the options. Before you leave, make sure you know the next step.

Prepare for your visit
  1. 1What changed?Symptoms, daily function, or anything you want to discuss.
  2. 2What matters to you?Share your priority and what you want to be able to do better.
  3. 3What happens next?Understand treatment, tests, follow-up and when to contact your team.

Patient perspectives

Patient questions help us make education more useful.

Patient experience highlights gaps in explanations and everyday support. We aim to learn from these perspectives while grounding medical information in evidence. Moderated peer support and research studies are future ambitions; creating an account does not enrol anyone in research.
