Newly diagnosed

Start with three things—not thirty pages.

A new lupus diagnosis can bring many questions. Focus first on understanding your own disease pattern, your treatment and your follow-up plan.

Important

This information is educational. It does not diagnose your condition or replace advice from your healthcare team.

Key information

1. Understand your lupus

Ask which symptoms or organs are involved in your case and which findings led to the diagnosis. Lupus differs from person to person.

Patient guide

2. Understand each medicine

Know why you take it, what benefit is expected, what monitoring is needed and which side effects or situations should prompt contact.

Patient guide

3. Know the follow-up plan

Before leaving a visit, know which tests are planned, when your next appointment is and when you should contact the team sooner.

Patient guide

Prepare your questions

Write down your three most important questions before the visit. Bring your medicine list and relevant previous results.

Patient guide

Avoid information overload

You do not need to master lupus immediately. Build understanding in steps and use reliable sources.

What should I do now?

Write down new or changing symptoms.Keep your medicines and recent results organized.Prepare your most important questions before your visit.Seek medical assessment promptly for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms.
