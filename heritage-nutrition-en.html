NUTRITION & EVERYDAY LIFE

From our heritage to our health

Emirati food and lupus: explore familiar dishes and what medical evidence says about their benefits and precautions.

🌴 🍯 🫒
The key message

No single diet treats lupus. Balanced eating supports general health, and kidney involvement, diabetes or high blood pressure may require tailored choices. Traditional remedies do not replace prescribed treatment.

From our Emirati table

Choose a food to read the evidence and precautions.

🌴
Traditional food

Dates

Benefits and precautions

Dates contain carbohydrates and fibre. Mind portions if you have diabetes. Potassium restrictions are not necessary for everyone with kidney disease.

🍯
Heritage & evidence

Honey

Benefits and precautions

Honey is mentioned in the Quran and contains sugars. There is insufficient clinical evidence that it treats lupus or prevents flares.

🌱
Supplement caution

Black seed

Benefits and precautions

Mentioned in Islamic tradition, but not proven to treat lupus. Discuss concentrated extracts and oils with your care team.

🫒
Food choice

Olive oil

Benefits and precautions

A source of unsaturated fat. Replacing some saturated fat with olive oil may support heart health, but it is not a lupus treatment.

🥣
Emirati cuisine

Harees

Benefits and precautions

Harees can be prepared with lean chicken and less salt and ghee. Portions and ingredients should be adapted to individual needs.

🍲
Emirati cuisine

Thareed & machboos

Benefits and precautions

Traditional meals can be enjoyed with more vegetables and mindful salt, fat and portion choices tailored to health needs.

🎧 The one-minute summary

Must people with lupus avoid dates or honey? Is black seed a proven treatment? There is no one-size-fits-all diet and no sufficient evidence these foods treat lupus. Choices depend on your health and medicines.

Before trying herbs or supplements

Speak to your clinician or pharmacist before taking supplements or concentrated extracts. Alfalfa sprouts and supplements are generally discouraged in lupus due to reports of lupus-like immune activation. Do not automatically restrict potassium or protein without medical advice.
