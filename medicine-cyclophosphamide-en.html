Specialist treatment for some severe cases

Cyclophosphamide

An intensive option for certain organ-threatening lupus manifestations requiring close monitoring.

Why might it be prescribed?

May be considered for certain severe kidney or other organ disease.

What makes this medicine different?

It may be delivered by specialist infusion protocols and is not chosen simply because symptoms are troublesome.

Before adding any medicineAsk your pharmacist to check interactions, including OTC medicines and supplements. Do not change treatment based on this page.

How it works: analogy and explanation
At the immune-cell 'factory,' severe problems may require a stronger intervention to limit cell multiplication, but with closer safety oversight.

It affects genetic material in dividing cells, including immune cells.

The analogy is a learning aid, not a literal description of biology.

How is it used and when may it help?

Schedule and route vary by specialist protocol, sometimes including intravenous infusions and bladder-protection measures.

If you miss a dose, follow the instructions for your specific product or ask your pharmacist. Do not double up without explicit advice.

Side effects and monitoring

Nausea, hair loss and fatigue can occur; low blood counts may increase infection risk.

Bladder, fertility and other organ harms are possible. Discuss fertility preservation before treatment and follow monitoring.

Which medicines may interact?

Review concurrent immune suppressants and vaccines with your team; combinations may heighten infection or blood-cell risks.

These are important examples, not a complete interaction list. Some combinations are intentional with monitoring; others may need avoidance. Bring your complete medicine list to your appointments.

Pregnancy, breastfeeding and family planning

Can harm pregnancy and fertility in women and men. Discuss contraception and fertility preservation before treatment.

When to get prompt or urgent medical help

Major fever, blood in urine, painful urination or new breathlessness require prompt medical assessment.

How does it compare with other medicines?
An option for selected more severe disease, but not automatically preferable to milder treatments for everyone.
