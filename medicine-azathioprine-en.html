Limits multiplication of some immune cells

Azathioprine

Reduces some immune-cell activity and may help longer-term disease control or limit steroid exposure.

Why might it be prescribed?

May be used for maintenance or selected organ manifestations depending on specialist planning.

What makes this medicine different?

It differs from mycophenolate's pathway and from methotrexate's usually weekly rheumatology schedule.

Before adding any medicineAsk your pharmacist to check interactions, including OTC medicines and supplements. Do not change treatment based on this page.

How it works: analogy and explanation
Imagine a factory making too many immune 'defenders.' Azathioprine slows access to building materials needed for cell multiplication. The same analogy helps with other medicines, but their mechanisms differ.

It is converted into compounds affecting synthesis of materials needed for cell division.

The analogy is a learning aid, not a literal description of biology.

How is it used and when may it help?

Typically taken by mouth as prescribed; it may take weeks to months to judge benefit.

If you miss a dose, follow the instructions for your specific product or ask your pharmacist. Do not double up without explicit advice.

Side effects and monitoring

Nausea, vomiting and stomach upset may occur, and blood count or liver problems can develop.

Regular blood and liver tests are required. Metabolism-related tests may be used. Report fever, unexpected bruising or bleeding.

Which medicines may interact?

Allopurinol and febuxostat, used for gout, can cause serious interactions; combinations may need avoidance or highly specialized adjustment.

These are important examples, not a complete interaction list. Some combinations are intentional with monitoring; others may need avoidance. Bring your complete medicine list to your appointments.

Pregnancy, breastfeeding and family planning

Unlike methotrexate and mycophenolate, azathioprine may be considered in some pregnancy plans under specialist guidance.

When to get prompt or urgent medical help

Contact your team urgently for significant fever, mouth ulcers, unexplained bleeding, bruising or jaundice.

How does it compare with other medicines?
Methotrexate is usually weekly and risky in pregnancy; mycophenolate is important for some kidney-disease plans but carries major pregnancy risk.
