Why these initiatives?

Patients as partners in knowledge, not only readers

We distinguish our published resources from future plans and do not present aspirations as completed programmes.

Our purpose

Make lupus information easier to understand and more relevant to daily life, without replacing personalised medical care.

Who we serve

People living with lupus, their families, and anyone looking for accessible, well-sourced information.

How we work

Start with a real need, use clear language, show the next practical step, and explain the evidence and review status.

Ideas under development

What we hope to build next

These are future concepts; this page does not offer registration or collect personal health information.

04Proposed

The patient's voice in editing

Invite patient readers to identify unclear explanations and missing practical questions. Any future collection of personal feedback would require a secure channel and appropriate safeguards.

05Proposed

Educational conversations about life with lupus

A concept for expert-led discussions based on patients' questions, subject to qualified involvement and medical review before any event is announced.

06Proposed

Patient priorities for knowledge and research

Explore ethical and protected ways to learn which questions matter most to patients, without collecting personal health data before safeguards are ready.

Our proposed approach

How an idea becomes an initiative

A transparent process that respects patient experience and editorial quality.

  1. 01Listen

    Identify everyday questions without requesting unnecessary health details.

  2. 02Explain

    Turn needs into understandable, actionable educational content.

  3. 03Review

    Check the evidence, safety and privacy before publication.

  4. 04Improve

    Update the material with documented feedback and clear review dates.
