About NA7N

Patient education designed for understanding and action.

NA7N is being built as a patient-first health platform. This lupus section aims to make reliable information easier to understand and use in conversations with healthcare professionals.

Important

This information is educational. It does not diagnose your condition or replace advice from your healthcare team.

Key information

How we write

We begin with the patient's question, identify the main message and practical next step, then build detail underneath. Important medical claims are grounded in appropriate sources.

Patient guide

Editorial standard

Our approach uses health-literacy principles including understandability and actionability. Medical accuracy, evidence, language, patient perspective, safety and actionability are reviewed separately.

Patient guide

Medical review status

We do not label a page medically reviewed unless a real medical review has occurred. Pages may therefore state that internal medical review is pending.

Patient guide

What NA7N does not do

NA7N does not diagnose an individual, prescribe treatment, change medication doses or interpret one laboratory result as a diagnosis.

Patient guide

Privacy in the current version

The current website is static. It does not currently provide patient accounts or store personal health histories. Future health-data features will require appropriate privacy, security and governance.
