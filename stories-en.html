Juzi: “My evidence”

Juzi is 30 years old and has lived with lupus for eight years. In a photography study, the women were asked to take pictures that show life with lupus. Juzi chose a collage of empty medicine boxes, bottles and injection materials.

Many women in the study described treatment as repetitive, tiring and hard to explain to other people. Juzi looked at the same objects in a different way. She said she likes collecting the boxes, because they show that she has kept going and kept fighting lupus.

“…proof that I’ve been persisting and fighting against lupus.”Juzi (a pseudonym), quoted in Xu, Xiang & Hou, 2026

The researchers describe this as a way of making the hidden work of treatment visible. Objects that could mean dependence or a damaged body became evidence of endurance.

What this story shows

The same treatment routine can carry more than one meaning. This is one woman’s way of understanding her own treatment. It is not advice, and not everyone feels this way.

A question for you: When you look at your own treatment routine, what does it show about your effort? You can bring your answer to your next visit.
