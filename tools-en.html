Patient tools

Turn what you notice into something you can use.

These tools help you organize meaningful changes, clarify what matters to you, and prepare for conversations with your healthcare team. They do not diagnose lupus activity, recommend treatment changes, or replace clinical assessment.

Privacy in the current version

NA7N is currently a static website. These tools are presented as prompts and templates; this version does not provide patient accounts or longitudinal health-data storage.

Tool 1 · Meaningful change record

Something changed—capture the useful part.

You do not need to record every sensation. Use this when something is new, persistent, clearly different, or important to your daily life.

What changed?
____________When did it start?
____________What pattern do I notice?
____________What happens with it?
____________How is it affecting my life?
____________What changed around the same time?
____________

Tool 2 · Quality-of-life priority

Choose one thing you want your care to help protect or improve.

What matters to me?
____________What is making it difficult?
____________What would “better” look like?
____________What help or decision do I need?
____________

Your goal can be practical: enough energy for family time, sleeping well enough to concentrate, continuing work or study, moving with less difficulty, or planning safely for pregnancy.

A single result may not explain your health by itself. Review results and trends in their clinical context rather than using this template for self-diagnosis.

Measurement boundary

These NA7N templates are not validated clinical questionnaires.

They are organizational and communication tools. NA7N will distinguish them from validated patient-reported outcome measures and will not present an unofficial translation, restricted instrument or informal score as a validated measure of lupus activity.

How health tracking works
Safety

Do not complete a form when you need medical assessment.

Severe or rapidly worsening symptoms require medical assessment rather than waiting to finish a tool or collect more information. Follow any individualized urgent-care instructions already provided by your healthcare team.
