Patient tools

Organize information before you need it.

These simple tools are designed to help you prepare for healthcare conversations. The current static website does not store the information you enter.

Important

This information is educational. It does not diagnose your condition or replace advice from your healthcare team.

Key information

Symptom log

Record the symptom, start date, duration, severity, impact, associated symptoms and possible context. Look for patterns rather than trying to diagnose the cause yourself.

Patient guide

Medication list

Keep medicine name, purpose, schedule as prescribed, and questions you want to ask. Include supplements and over-the-counter products.

Patient guide

Tests list

Record the test name, date, why it was ordered and the question you want answered. Compare trends with your clinician rather than interpreting isolated numbers.

Patient guide

Three-question card

Before an appointment, choose the three questions that matter most. This helps protect time for your priorities.

Patient guide

Privacy

The current version is a static website and does not provide patient accounts or longitudinal health-data storage.
