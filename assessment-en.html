Track your health

Patient-reported information can support better conversations.

Patient-reported outcome tools can help organize what you experience and follow change over time. They do not diagnose lupus activity or replace clinical assessment.

Important

This information is educational. It does not diagnose your condition or replace advice from your healthcare team.

Key information

What a patient-reported result means

A score reflects what you reported through that instrument. A higher score does not automatically mean medically assessed lupus activity has increased.

Patient guide

Look at trends

Repeated information over time may be more useful for discussion than interpreting one reading in isolation.

Patient guide

Prepare for your visit

Use patient-reported information to identify what changed and what you want to discuss with your clinician.

Patient guide

Do not change treatment from a score

Do not start, stop or adjust medicine based only on a questionnaire result.

Patient guide

Current status

Some assessment tools require permission, validated translations or instrument-specific scoring materials. Ma3i will not present an unofficial translation as a validated instrument.
