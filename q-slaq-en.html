    How the three scores are calculated

    Severity (0–34): computed using the Swedish Rheumatology Quality Register Q-SLAQ grouping rules. Certain related symptoms are grouped, not simply added as 18 independent severity items.

    Symptom count (0–18): each symptom present is counted once, irrespective of how severe it was.

    Patient global activity (0–10): an independent rating of how active the disease felt to you during the previous month.
